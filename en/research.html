🔬 Research Guide

📋 What to Research First in Crashlanded

In the base-game Crashlanded scenario, Electricity, Air Conditioning, and Stonecutting are already researched. Start with whichever problem is most urgent: power, food storage, or equipment. This guide was checked against the base-game definitions in 1.6.4871.

ResearchWhen to Consider ItWhat It Unlocks or Requires
Electricity, Air Conditioning, and StonecuttingAlready researchedBuild a power supply, cool a freezer, or cut stone blocks as needed.
Battery and Solar PanelPower storage or generationBattery stores power. Solar Panel supplies daytime power. Each project requires Electricity.
MachiningEquipmentRequires Electricity and Smithing. Unlocks the machining table and is a prerequisite for Multi-analyzer.
MicroelectronicsResearch facilitiesUnlocks the hi-tech research bench and comms console. Requires Electricity, so you can research it before or after Machining.
Multi-analyzerComponent productionFinish Machining and Microelectronics, then research this at a hi-tech research bench. Build the multi-analyzer where it can link to that bench.
FabricationComponent productionUse a hi-tech research bench with a linked multi-analyzer to research Fabrication. It unlocks the fabrication bench.
Geothermal PowerA useful steam geyserRequires Electricity. Research it when you have a suitable geyser and can build a generator there.
💡 Before you start: Save 200 steel, 12 components, and 2 advanced components to build the fabrication bench. Making components at the bench requires Crafting 8.

🔄 Research by Starting Scenario

Start TypeWhat to PrioritizeKey Difference
Crashlanded Use the starting technologies, then research what the colony needs for power or equipment. Three colonists start with Air Conditioning, so they can build a freezer as soon as they can power it.
Lost Tribe Choose clothing, furniture, or electricity based on the climate, food storage, and defenses. The tribe starts at the Neolithic tech level, so higher-level projects cost more. Use what is already available to meet immediate needs.
The Rich Explorer Cover the lone colonist's immediate survival and defenses, then choose equipment or research based on supplies. Check the Research tab for completed projects before picking the next one.

🗺️ Research Path to Component Production

  1. Machining and Microelectronics: Finish both in either order. Build the hi-tech research bench unlocked by Microelectronics.
  2. Multi-analyzer: Research it at the hi-tech research bench, then build the analyzer and link it to the bench.
  3. Fabrication: Research it at the analyzer-equipped bench. Build a fabrication bench to make components.
  4. Advanced Fabrication: Research it after Fabrication using the same research setup. Then make advanced components at the fabrication bench.

Bionic Replacements branches off after Fabrication and also requires Prosthetics. For ship technology, research Starflight Basics after Advanced Fabrication.

⚡ Research Speed Tips

  • Keep the research room clean. Sterile tile can help you raise room cleanliness.
  • Connect a multi-analyzer to a hi-tech research bench. It speeds up research and is required for projects such as Fabrication.
  • Set research job priorities so a researcher has time at the bench. Use their displayed research speed to choose who works on important projects.
  • Light the room and check that a hi-tech research bench has power.
  • Use additional research benches if more than one colonist can contribute to the selected project.
