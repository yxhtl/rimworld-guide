Sources and checking method

This is a versioned research guide, not a personal-playthrough diary. Core pages show the game version, last-check date, and links to the references used.

Source levels

  • Level A: developer and official Steam announcements for versions, patches, and copyright information.
  • Level B: RimWorld Wiki mechanics and data pages, rechecked after major game updates.
  • Level C: public community discussion, used only to frame player choices and never as the sole support for a factual claim.

Editorial rules

  • Facts, versions, and numbers need a traceable source. Conflicts are flagged instead of smoothed over.
  • Recommendations state their assumptions and are not presented as a single best answer.
  • The site does not reuse Wiki, video, or other creators' screenshots. Visuals are original diagrams or explicitly permitted marks.
