🏥 Surgery & Organs

💉 Three Medicine Types — Never Use Herbal for Surgery

MedicinePotencySurgery MultiplierUse For
🌿 Herbal60%×0.4Minor wounds, daily treatment (spoils in 150 days)
💊 Industrial100%×0.7Standard surgeries. Craftable, commonly sold
✨ Glitterworld160%×1.0+High-risk surgeries, bionic/archotech installation
⚠️ Hard rule: Never use herbal medicine for surgery—the ×0.4 multiplier tanks success below safe thresholds. Industrial for routine ops, Glitterworld for critical ones.

🎯 Surgery Success — More Than Just Medical Skill

Final success = Doctor's Surgery Capability × Bed Factor × Medicine Potency × Procedure Difficulty. Hard cap at 98%—can never reach 100%.

  • Doctor skill (most important): Medical 10 = ~90% base. Each level above 10 adds ~2%. Skill 14+ for reliable surgery
  • Manipulation: Bionic arms (125%), Archotech arms (150%) directly boost surgical precision
  • Sight: Bionic eyes (125%), Archotech eyes (150%)—dual archotech eyes make a huge difference
  • Room cleanliness: Sterile tiles (+0.6 cleanliness) significantly improve outcomes. Dirty room = malpractice
  • Lighting: Darkness reduces sight → reduces success. Always operate in full light

🫀 Organ Harvesting — Profit With a Price

Harvestable organs: Heart (most valuable), lungs, kidneys, liver, stomach.

Mood cost: −6 colony-wide ×6 days per harvest. Surgeon gets separate −6. Stacks per organ. Ideoligions that accept organ harvesting negate these penalties.

Practicality: Organs are extremely profitable early-game. But mood management must keep pace—either have an accepting ideoligion or restrict harvesting to death-row prisoners only.

⚠️ Surgery Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Herbal medicine for surgery: ×0.4 multiplier = nearly guaranteed failure. Herbal is for basic treatment only
  2. Dirty operating room: Cleanliness directly tanks success rate. Keep the OR spotless
  3. Operating in the dark: Sight penalty → lower success. Always have full lighting
  4. Low-skill surgeon: Medical <8 doing surgery is gambling with patients' lives. Train to 10+ first
  5. Patient not resting: Patient must be in a medical bed on "Bed Rest" priority, and doctor must be available—or the operation never happens
  6. Wrong medicine policy: Prisoners default to herbal-only. If your glitterworld meds aren't in their allowed list, the doctor won't use them
