🧩 Why pawns idle: find the one missing condition instead of scrambling the whole Work tab

1. First, confirm that the pawn is actually available to work

“Idle” does not always mean something is broken. Select the pawn and check their current state: drafted, downed, on a mental break, or meeting food, rest, recreation, or other urgent needs. Separate that state from “no valid job exists” before changing settings.

What you seeWhat it meansDo this first
Drafted, downed, or in a mental breakNormal Work-tab jobs will not runUndraft, rescue, or resolve the active crisis
Eating, resting, or recreatingA need or schedule is temporarily ahead of workLet the current action finish, then see whether idling continues
No prioritize option appears when you right-clickAt least one condition for that pawn or target failsTry a suitable pawn, then check work, access, and the target
💡 Fast isolation: pick one clear blueprint, crop, or bench and right-click it with a suitable pawn. Whether a valid action appears tells you more than the fact that the pawn looks idle.

2. Work tab: enabled is not immediate

In manual priorities, a blank box disables the work type and smaller numbers are more urgent. Within the same priority, a pawn checks work types from left to right, so Construction can be enabled yet continuously lose to hauling, growing, care, or another available job.

  1. Confirm the work type is not blank: Construction for blueprints, Cooking for meals, Crafting for production, and Growing for sowing or harvest.
  2. Find higher or equal-priority competition: temporarily raise the work you need instead of setting the whole row to 1.
  3. Check incapabilities: a backstory restriction is not low skill; that pawn cannot take that work at all.
  4. Change one cause at a time: observe whether the pawn begins, otherwise you will not know which setting mattered.

For the normal division of labor, manual mode, and colony-size setups, use Work Priorities. This page only answers why the current job is not running.

3. Areas, doors, and paths: a target can exist without being usable

Allowed areas affect where a pawn can work. Locked doors, restricted zones, collapsed passages, threats, extreme conditions, and sealed rooms can also leave a task without a usable route. Check the pawn, target tile, and required materials together.

CheckTypical blockHow to verify the fix
PawnAssigned to an indoor, medical, or temporary safety areaTemporarily make them unrestricted, or include the target in their current area
TargetBehind a door, in a restricted zone, or in an unreachable roomRight-click it and look for the actual work option
MaterialsForbidden, behind a door, or outside the usable routeCheck that both the pawn and materials are reachable and allowed
⚠️ Do not only inspect the painted area: a pawn can cross a restricted space to travel, but cannot interact with a target outside their allowed area. Check the task tile and material location.

4. Blueprints, materials, and valid targets: no valid job means no work

Construction, harvest, and hauling often stall because there is no task that can currently finish. A blueprint can lack materials, a crop may not be ready, an item may be forbidden, or another pawn may already own that exact task.

  • Construction: select the blueprint and confirm material quantity, permitted material, and a reachable build tile.
  • Growing and harvest: confirm there is a mature crop or a sowable tile; pawns do not work outside their allowed area.
  • Hauling: confirm the item is allowed and a stockpile, shelf, or task destination accepts it.
  • One exact target: one concrete job normally has one worker; test a nearby separate target instead of sending several pawns to the same tile.

For a production-chain problem, continue to Crafting & Production. If a freezer, kitchen, or appliance failure is causing the chain reaction, stabilize Power and Food emergency first.

5. A workbench will not run: inspect the bill, not only the bench

A bench exists and Craft or Cook is enabled does not mean work will begin. Pawns attempt active bills they qualify for. A suspended bill, satisfied quantity, ingredient filter, ingredient radius, skill or worker filter can make every bill get skipped.

  1. Open Bills: confirm the bill is not suspended and its target quantity is not already met.
  2. Check ingredient filters and radius: having an item is not enough if it does not match the bill or sits outside the allowed search radius.
  3. Check product conditions: for “do until you have X”, inspect current products, storage, and any quality condition.
  4. Check device conditions: restore fuel or power for equipment that needs it; use Power emergency for an outage.
  5. Force one test: right-click the bench with a suitable pawn. The in-game failure message is more reliable than guessing.
✅ A completed diagnosis: you can name who will do one concrete task, where they get materials, which path they use, and which bill or order creates the result. It is not “everyone is now priority 1.”
