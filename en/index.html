🧯 Emergency first aid

When the colony is about to lose control, open the guide that matches the immediate threat.

🍲 Food emergency

Secure the next meal, then restore harvesting, hauling and cooking.

⚡ Power emergency

Check appliances, connections and demand; keep climate, care and food online.

🔥 Fire emergency

Rescue first, contain second; check heat and exits indoors.

☣️ Toxic fallout

Move into roofed safety first, then protect animals, food, and recovery.

🛡️ Defense check

Assemble and hold first; rescue and repair after the threat ends.

🧠 Mental break response

Limit risky behavior, then repair the fastest mood trigger.
