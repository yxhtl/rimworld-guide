😊 Mood System

🧠 How Mood Works

Open the colonist’s Needs tab and compare the mood bar with their break markers. The base thresholds are 35% for minor, 20% for major, and 5% for extreme. Traits and other effects can move those markers. Spending time below one raises the risk of a break.

Base mood rangeRisk from low mood
35% or higherAbove the base break thresholds
20% to under 35%Minor break, such as sad wandering or a food binge
5% to under 20%Major break, such as a tantrum
Under 5%Extreme break, such as berserk or murderous rage

Before a break: Check the active thoughts and unmet needs. Handle immediate problems such as hunger, exhaustion, pain, or unsafe temperatures first. Then address recurring problems such as meals without a table or a lack of recreation.

Checked against RimWorld 1.6.4871 base-game definitions and the game’s break-threshold logic.

🏠 Room Impressiveness—The Underrated Mood Engine

Room impressiveness combines wealth, beauty, space, and cleanliness. Check the room’s rating and its lowest stat before adding furniture. The mood thought also depends on what the colonist did in the room.

Room ratingDining or recreation thoughtBedroom thought
AwfulNone−2
DullNoneNone
MediocreNone+1
Decent+2+2
Slightly impressive+3+3
Somewhat impressive+4+4
Very impressive+5+5
Extremely impressive+6+6
Unbelievably impressive+7+7
Wondrously impressive+8+8

Shared dining and recreation room: Eating and doing recreation can grant separate thoughts in the same room. If both thoughts are active at the top rating, they add up to +16 mood.

  • 8×8 to 12×12, centrally located
  • 1–2 high-quality large sculptures (art is the best mood investment)
  • Stone tile floors, armchairs (not stools) at tables
  • Keep it clean. Filth can lower the room’s rating. Recheck the rating after cleaning

🛏️ Bedrooms

  • Size: 5×5 internal (25 tiles) optimal. 4×5 can reach "decent"
  • Furnishings: Best bed + dresser + end table + lamp + at least one sculpture or plant pot
  • Special traits: Greedy pawns need "slightly impressive"; Jealous pawns need the best bedroom; Ascetics hate nice rooms

🎮 Recreation Management

  • Variety is mandatory: One horseshoe pin = slow rec gain. At least 3 different recreation types
  • Types: Horseshoes (dexterity), chess (cerebral), billiards (dexterity + social), TV (social), telescope (cerebral), poker (social)
  • Schedule: 2–3 hours daily, after waking and mid-afternoon
  • Recreation tolerance: Same activity loses effectiveness over time—rotate activities

💊 Drug Mood Policy—Automated Safety Net

Psychite tea and beer can give temporary mood boosts. Check the item’s safe-dose interval before scheduling use. Consider the colonist’s age, body size, tolerance, addiction, and chemical traits.

💡 If you use a schedule: Set the interval and mood condition in the drug policy, and check whether social use is also enabled. Psychite tea and other psychite drugs share tolerance. Use food, sleep, and recreation to address recurring low mood.

That free +30 you get at the start? It's a tutorial buff. By 200k wealth you must replace it with better rooms, food, art, and recreation. Wealth management matters because rising expectations outpace comfort if you climb too fast.

✅ Mood Management Checklist

  1. Combine dining + rec into one great hall with 2 large sculptures
  2. 5×5 bedrooms with bed + dresser + end table + lamp + plant pot
  3. Armchairs everywhere—workstations and dining tables (+10 comfort)
  4. Fine meals minimum, lavish when affordable (+5 to +12)
  5. Review any drug policy against the pawn’s needs, health and the item’s safe-dose interval
  6. At least 3 recreation types, 2 hours scheduled daily
  7. Sculptures everywhere—workshops, labs, hallways. Best beauty-to-wealth ratio
  8. Keep it clean—filth is the #1 impressiveness killer
  9. Hide corpses—isolate battlefields and graves from living areas
  10. Check the danger before intervening in an active mental break
