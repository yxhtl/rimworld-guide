🚀 Odyssey gravships: being able to launch is not a reason to launch

1. First, confirm this is your playstyle

Odyssey is DLC content. Without it, ordinary caravans, transport pods, and quest travel still belong in Trade & Economy and Quest Decisions. A gravship moves the colony itself: structures, people, animals, prisoners, and stock can all leave together. The decision is not merely whether to visit a tile, but whether the colony can move its survival system.

Ask firstIf the answer is noSafer alternative
Does this trip solve a real problem?You only want to see a new placeStabilize food, medical care, and defense at home first
Can cooking, treatment, and power still run after launch?One pawn holds a critical roleAdd coverage or reduce the trip; do not gamble the last doctor or cook
Have you read the destination risk?You only saw the lootPause on the world map and read the quest, faction, and location text
✅ Launch when: the trip has a specific supply, quest, or location purpose, and a bad landing will not simultaneously remove food, medicine, and retreat options.

2. Before launch: four survival lines

LineCheck nowCommon failure
Flight and returnCurrent ship UI for connection, propulsion, fuel, range, and warningsBudgeting only for arrival, with no margin for recovery or diversion
Food and medical careEdible food, medicine, beds, and someone able to treat injuriesLeaving the working stock behind, then landing without cooking or treatment
Power and living areaWhether critical systems, sleep, storage, and work can operate firstBuilding a large workshop before refrigeration, rest, and care exist
Risk exitCombat, weather, factions, and withdrawal conditions at the destinationTreating a reconnaissance trip as a fight you must finish

Exact structures, fuel, range, and landing restrictions can change with patches, mods, settings, and the ship UI. Use the live highlight, warnings, and range information as final.

3. Choose a minimum running crew, not only fighters

RoleWhy it mattersWhat to do without it
Treatment / haulingLanding combat, illness, and accidents immediately reduce action capacityLower the destination risk or add medicine and backup coverage first
Cooking / food sourceA mobile colony cannot live only on one packed meal stackCarry realistic shelf-life and return margin; do not treat a long stay as a day trip
Building / powerThe first night needs safe basic facilitiesDo not fill every slot with combat roles
Home teamIf an old base remains, it can still face raids, injuries, and outagesKeep medical care, cooking, firefighting, and defendable rooms at home

For prisoners use Prisoners & Recruitment; animals also need food, enclosure, and safe routes after landing.

4. The first hour after landing: survive before looting

  1. Read the ground: identify hostile targets, a safe rest area, weather, and a withdrawal direction; do not unload everyone blindly.
  2. Restore the survival line: make treatment, food, sleep, and essential power work before optional production.
  3. Choose the job: decide whether this is rescue, trade, exploration, or attack. If the goal stops being worthwhile, people and ship come first.
⚠️ A temporary map is not home: set a safe area before expanding hauling, gathering, or construction on unfamiliar routes.

5. When to enter orbit: breathing, power, and retreat are the gate

Orbital sites are higher-risk Odyssey content. Official material lists sealed rooms, life support, stable power, airlocks, and vacuum protection as preparation; owning a ship is not the same as being ready for space.

ConditionIf it is missingCorrect action now
Sealing and life supportPawns face vacuum dangerReturn to ground play and stabilize the ship living area first
Stable power and repair marginA failed critical system becomes an evacuationReduce variable load and carry repair / emergency stock
Controllable combat and retreatOrbital facilities can have guards and narrow routesUse Combat & Defense to assess it as a high-risk quest
