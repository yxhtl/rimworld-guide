🔒 Privacy Policy

Straightforward, no beating around the bush

📌 In a Nutshell

You can read the guides without creating an account, signing in, or filling in personal details. Our hosting and analytics providers may process request and usage data during a visit. Voluntary feedback is handled by an external form.

🖥️ Hosting Service: Vercel

Vercel hosts the production site at rimworld.yxhtl.com. It may process request information such as IP addresses to respond to requests, deliver pages, and operate the service. See the Vercel Privacy Notice.

📊 Site Analytics

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand page visits and guide use, including page paths, language, table-of-contents clicks, source-link clicks, and reading depth. We do not intentionally add account details, names, or feedback text to these custom analytics events. See Google Analytics data safeguards.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Helps us understand interactions such as clicks and scrolling and may generate session replays. Microsoft says Clarity can infer approximate location from IP addresses. See the Clarity FAQ and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Baidu Analytics: Helps us understand page visits and traffic sources. Baidu Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to process visit data. See the Baidu Analytics Privacy Policy.

📋 Feedback Form: Tencent Survey

This site collects feedback via Tencent Survey (wj.qq.com). When you submit feedback, Tencent Survey processes the data according to its own privacy policy — we only see the content you fill in.

The feedback form is optional — skipping it doesn't affect your ability to read the guides.

🍪 LocalStorage

This site uses your browser's localStorage to remember your theme preference (dark/light mode). This data:

  • Only exists in your own browser
  • Is never uploaded to any server
  • Can be removed by clearing this site's data in your browser

The analytics services above may use their own cookies or similar technologies. You can manage site data in your browser settings.

📢 Advertising status

This site does not currently run Google AdSense or another ad network. If advertising is introduced later, this notice will be updated first and an appropriate consent choice will be provided where required.

🔗 External Links

This site contains links to external websites (such as Steam, Ludeon Studios, Mod pages, etc.). Once you click these links, your privacy on those sites is governed by their respective privacy policies, not by this site.

  • This site's guides are based on RimWorld 1.4+ with all DLC. Future updates may make some content outdated.
  • RimWorld and all its DLC are copyright Ludeon Studios. This site is a fan-made community guide, unaffiliated with Ludeon Studios.
